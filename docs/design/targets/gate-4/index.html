Gate 4 design target: the signed-in app

The look every app screen uses from now on, shown on the shipper's Home (S-01). Open Home full size or the component sheet. Narrow the window below 1024 px for the phone layout.

  • Desktop: a 240 px white sidebar with the role's main action at the top (Book a truck), then the nav, with settings and the signed-in person at the bottom. A 64 px top bar holds the page title (on Home, the greeting instead), a search pill with ⌘K, the bell and the account switcher. Below that: a main column (Needs your action, Live trips table) and a 360 px right rail (Book a truck, Book again, This month).
  • Phone: a top bar with the account initials, the greeting (Home has no page title, since the Home tab already says where you are) and the bell. In order: Needs your action, Book a truck, Book again (swipe), live trip cards. The 5 bottom tabs sit in a floating white pill (45 px radius, 12 px from the sides and bottom, soft shadow) with the raised green Book button.
  • Other roles: the same frame. Only the tabs, the sidebar items and the main action change (owner: Find loads; owner-driver: Drive; driver: 4 bigger tabs). See the component sheet.
  • Amounts show in the app (₦630,000 held safely). D031 only covers the public website.
