How you earn

You earn 1% of the trip price on every completed trip by a truck owner or sender you bring, for their first 6 months.

Paid by Freight Africa, so they never pay more because of you. They sign up and check their own ID. You're paid when each trip closes.

Example
A trader you bring sends a load to Lagos for ₦1,000,000. When it's delivered, you earn ₦10,000. If a truck owner you brought carries it too, you earn on both sides.
