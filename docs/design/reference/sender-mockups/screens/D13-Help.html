How does offering my own price work?

You pay the price you offer, and we hold it safely. Verified owners on your route see it. They can accept it, or reply with their own price. If they reply, you choose to pay the difference, offer again once, or decline. If nobody agrees within 24 hours, you can raise your offer, change the day, or get every naira back.
